OVERVIEW: Move a tab to a slot, to another project, or both.

The slot is the final position of the tab in the `tab list` order, counted from
1. With --to-project, the tab goes to the end of the tabs of that project, and
the slot is in that project.

USAGE: macterm tab move <tab> [<slot>] [--to-project <to-project>] [--project <project>] [--socket <socket>] [--json]

ARGUMENTS:
  <tab>                   Tab title, UUID, or index (tab:3).
  <slot>                  Destination slot: the position of the tab at the end,
                          counted from 1.

OPTIONS:
  --to-project <to-project>
                          Destination project (name, UUID, or index).
  --project <project>     Project scope. The default is the active project.
  --socket <socket>       Control socket path. It overrides discovery.
  --json                  Print the raw JSON payload instead of a table.
  -h, --help              Show help information.
